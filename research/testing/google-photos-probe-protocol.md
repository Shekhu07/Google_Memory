# Google Photos search test (about 15 minutes)

**Goal:** see whether Google Photos can find a specific old photo when you only half-remember it.

## Steps

1. **Pick 6 photos, each at least a year old:** 3 gym, 2 wedding, 1 festival. Scroll to each one first, so you know exactly which photo you're looking for.
2. **For each photo, write down when you *think* it was taken,** in your own words ("when I started bulking"). Then swipe up on the photo to see the real date.
3. **Search twice for each photo:**
   - **Search A:** the one word you'd type first: `gym`, `wedding`, `diwali`
   - **Search B:** how you'd naturally describe it: `gym photo from when I was bulking`, `haldi at [cousin]'s wedding`, `pichle saal diwali`
4. **Note one result per search:**
   - **top:** on the first screen
   - **found:** after scrolling a bit
   - **no:** not found

   If it's a **no**, add a few words on why ("hundreds of gym pics", "nothing came up").

That's it: 12 searches.
